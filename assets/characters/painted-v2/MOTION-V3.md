# Motion and combat v3

Six supplementary atlases: `public/images/rooftop-<id>-motion-v3.png`.
Generated by the built-in image tool from each corresponding v2 reference.
Actual output dimensions: 1254 x 1254, four columns and four rows. Source cells
are calculated from the actual dimensions; no hardcoded 384-pixel crop is used.

Frames 0-7: running cycle. Frames 8-15: ready, draw back, windup, swing,
impact, follow-through, return, recovery. Existing v2 idle, guard, recoil and
victory poses remain available. This is frame-based animation, not motion capture.

Prompt: preserve the exact reference character, face, outfit and held weapon.
Create a 4x4 full-body animation atlas with consistent scale and feet baseline,
eight alternating-foot running frames followed by eight sequential attack
frames. Face right in three-quarter view. Keep all weapons inside their cells.
True alpha transparency, no background, shadows, grid, text or numbers.
Requested size was 1536 square; the tool returned 1254 square.

Combat: 180 HP, 120-second rounds, two wins per match. Light attacks cause short
recoil; third confirmed hit increases damage and knockback. Heavy attack costs
24 energy, breaks frontal guard and knocks down. Dodge costs 25, grants 240 ms
invulnerability and can cancel recovery. Jump costs 16. Guard costs 12 plus
energy on impact. Arena boundaries are solid; no shrink or ring-out victory.
Timeout uses highest remaining HP, with equal leaders drawing the round.

Tests cover damage timing, interrupted startup, guard, heavy guard break,
invulnerability, jumps, energy, combos, KO, bounds, timeout, match wins and
20 seeded matches. Browser scripts exercise all six characters and four sizes.
